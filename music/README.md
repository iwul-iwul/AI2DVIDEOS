# Your own background music (optional)

Reelhouse composes a short tune for every story by itself, so this folder can stay empty.

To use your own songs instead, put music files here (MP3, WAV, OGG or M4A). Reelhouse picks one for each story and plays it quietly under the voices.

To match songs to the story's mood, put them in folders named after the moods Reelhouse uses: `playful`, `tender`, `mystery`, `adventure` and `dreamy`. For example `music/dreamy/lullaby.mp3`. A story with no matching folder uses the songs here at the top.

Only use music you have the rights to post. Free options include the YouTube Audio Library and music marked CC0 or "no attribution required".
